# OpenHK50 Android design

- **Today** leads with today’s step total in the band’s local time zone. The total opens Steps history for that date. The activity dial uses bright red for band calories, bright yellow for workout time, and bright blue for steps. Distance sits beneath the dial. Show the latest heart rate and its timestamp on Today, updating in place during a check. Last night’s sleep opens its daily history. Seven-day averages use the previous seven complete local dates with readings.
- **Daily activity** leads with estimated calorie burn and uses the resting-calories row to edit BMR. A date picker and week strip browse past days. Group band calories, workout time, and steps in aligned rows with time-of-day charts where data is available.
- **Navigation** groups Today, Workouts, Health, and Band in a floating Liquid Glass-inspired bar on phones and a rail on wide windows. Use transparent, refractive glass with a reflective rim and a colored lens that stretches as it moves between tabs. Keep tab hit targets in place. History returns to the originating tab. Camera remote and Phone clock use dedicated views.
- **Motion** carries tabs and details along the direction of travel, and brings pop-ups and period changes in vertically. The activity dial sweeps from its displayed progress to new readings. A solid moving indicator shows an active spot check. Preserve scroll position during refreshes and when returning to a tab. Play route transitions only for navigation and honor Android’s animation setting.
- **Copy** starts with useful data or the primary action. Give each destination one navigation label. Omit repeated tab titles, greetings, and decorative weekday or date headers. Use dates where they identify a history range or workout record.
- **Appearance** follows Android’s theme. Use a true-black dark canvas (`#000000`) and an ice-blue light canvas. Use solid colors for all panels, buttons, and charts. Reserve translucency, gradients, and refraction for navigation. Use saturated red, yellow, and blue for metric graphics and primary actions, with higher-contrast foregrounds in light mode. Keep native system bars, permissions, back handling, and font scaling.
- **Data** uses HK50 daily totals and interval charts, converts band energy to kcal, and uses workout minutes from completed phone sessions. Workout time excludes pauses and is apportioned to the local days it crosses. Personal builds show estimated active calories on live and saved Strength sessions. Empty readings display a dash. History retains day, week, and year drill-down and keeps spot checks separate from historical averages.
- **Health** starts with a one-tap heart-rate check. The check continues across tabs and while Android is in the background. Show Stop in the measurement view and its notification. Keep spot checks out of daily averages and overnight baselines. Show recovery and strain with their available inputs, and draw ECG waveforms from imported voltage samples.
- **Band** starts with connection and sync, followed by nearby devices during discovery and grouped rows for tools, alerts, settings, privacy, and backup. The Band call alerts switch updates in place. Phone vibration offers app search and per-app switches after notification access is granted. The tested HK50 has no screen or button. The phone provides the digital and analog clocks and workout controls. The wrist shutter works while Camera remote is open through the confirmed camera-mode event. See [HK50 hardware and protocol evidence](docs/reference/HK50.md).

## Apple references

- Use the floating navigation and color guidance from Liquid Glass with Android’s system controls and accessibility settings. [Liquid Glass materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials)
- Fitness puts daily progress, steps, distance, recent sessions, and trends in Summary. Apple’s Stand metric requires detecting standing and movement by hour. OpenHK50 uses steps as its third goal. [Fitness Summary](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/iphone/iph4c34a8a95/ios)
- Apple groups workouts by activity and provides pause, resume, and end controls. Sleep exposes the previous night, history, and stage breakdowns. [Workout guide](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/watch/apdd16e8761a/watchos) · [Sleep guide](https://support.apple.com/en-md/guide/watch/crpxel1lvun9/watchos)
- Apple’s Watch companion manages devices, faces, and app arrangement. On the watch, the screen and Digital Crown handle navigation. OpenHK50 keeps device tools together and lets users switch the phone clock between digital and analog. [Watch setup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109015) · [Face Gallery](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/watch/apda6559ad78/watchos) · [App arrangement](https://support.apple.com/en-my/guide/watch/apd3cd8641c2/watchos) · [Watch controls](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105063)
- Apple’s Camera Remote uses the watch to frame and trigger photos. OpenHK50 uses the phone preview with a wrist shutter, optional three-second countdown, and phone camera selection. [Camera Remote guide](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/watch/apda6e61c287/watchos)
